# Contributing to AI Read All Books

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **AI Read All Books**!

AI Read All Books is an open educational project dedicated to organizing and developing comprehensive learning resources across a wide range of academic disciplines. Contributions from readers, researchers, students, and developers are welcome.

1. Reporting Errors and Suggesting Improvements

If you discover an error, outdated information, or an area that could be improved, please open an issue on GitHub.

When submitting an issue, try to include:

- The name and path of the relevant file.
- The original passage or information that needs attention.
- A clear explanation of the problem.
- Suggested corrections, if available.
- Reliable references or sources supporting your suggestion.

For historical dates, scientific claims, linguistic relationships, translations, and other specialized information, please provide sources whenever possible.

2. Contributing Content

You are welcome to improve existing chapters or contribute new educational materials.

When editing or creating Markdown files, please follow these guidelines:

- Maintain the existing directory structure and naming conventions.
- Use clear and descriptive headings.
- Organize content into logically structured sections.
- Provide sufficient explanations rather than relying solely on brief summaries.
- Include relevant examples, illustrations, tables, and references where appropriate.
- Maintain academic accuracy and distinguish established findings from hypotheses and ongoing debates.
- Update the relevant table of contents when adding new chapters.

3. Academic Standards and References

The project aims to provide reliable, detailed, and academically informed educational resources.

Contributors should:

- Prioritize academic publications, university resources, scholarly books, and reputable research institutions.
- Provide citations for factual claims and quoted material.
- Clearly identify uncertainty, competing interpretations, and unresolved academic debates.
- Avoid presenting speculation as established fact.
- Preserve original-language examples and provide appropriate translations when relevant.

4. Copyright and Licensing

Only submit materials that you have the right to contribute and distribute.

Please ensure that:

- Your original contributions are appropriately identified.
- Third-party texts, images, diagrams, and other materials are properly attributed.
- Any applicable copyright restrictions and licensing requirements are respected.
- You do not upload copyrighted materials without appropriate permission or a compatible license.

The project's license does not automatically apply to third-party materials included in the repository.

5. Submitting Changes

To contribute changes:

Fork the repository.

Create a branch for your proposed changes.

Edit or add the relevant Markdown files.

Review your changes for accuracy, formatting, and completeness.

Submit a pull request describing what you changed and why.

Please keep pull requests focused and provide references for significant factual corrections.

6. Review Process

All contributions are subject to review by the project maintainers.

Maintainers may request revisions, clarification, additional references, or formatting adjustments before accepting a contribution.

The final decision to merge contributions rests with the project maintainers.

7. Our Goal

AI Read All Books is intended to be a growing, collaborative knowledge resource covering the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, mathematics, technology, and other fields of human knowledge.

We welcome thoughtful contributions that help make knowledge more accessible, organized, accurate, and comprehensive.

Thank you for helping build AI Read All Books!
